Storage units

How big is each storage unit and what can I store in it?

Each storage unit measures 8′ wide, 20′ long, and 8′ high—about the size of a standard single-car garage. That’s 160 sq. ft. of floor space and over 5,000 cubic ft. of storage capacity. With smart organisation, such as racking or bins, you can store a large volume of belongings while keeping aisles clear for easy access. These units are perfect for household goods, business inventory, tools, seasonal items, recreational gear, and more—essentially, anything you can dream up.

What can I store in a container?

Start by identifying items you’ll only use during certain times of the year. These seasonal items can be rotated in and out of storage as needed, keeping your home uncluttered while ensuring everything is on hand when the season changes. It’s a smart way to stay organized and make the most of your space.

Are the units weather proof?

Our storage units are built to high-quality construction standards. They are completely moisture-free and rodent-free, giving you peace of mind year-round.

Are the storage units secure and insulated?

Yes. The site has 24/7 security with cameras, gated access and secure locks, and every 8′ x 20′ container is vented and spray-foamed for insulation.

Renting a unit

Do you offer short-term and long-term rentals?

Yes, we provide both flexible short-term rentals for temporary needs and long-term leases for ongoing storage.

How do I rent a storage unit?

Call Local Boys Storage at 705-444-4953 or send a message through our contact form, and we’ll help you reserve the right unit.

Do I need to take out insurance?

Yes, we recommend that you secure your own insurance to protect your belongings while in storage.

Do I need to provide a lock for my storage unit?

While you’re welcome to bring your own lock, we also offer high-quality locks for purchase at our facility.
